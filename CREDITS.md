## Sources for concepts

- "Third place", Wikipedia — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_place — Oldenburg's characteristics (welcoming, nearby, a leveller, regulars, conversation as the main activity), which map onto the model's place attributes.
- Pune365, on Pune's katta tradition — https://pune365.com/?p=48108 — the katta as a roadside gathering for debate, largely ended by traffic. Basis for the "Traffic took the kattas" scenario.
- Duncan & Duncan (1955), index of dissimilarity; Watts & Strogatz (1998), clustering coefficient; Granovetter (1973), weak ties; McFadden, multinomial logit choice; common random numbers as a Monte Carlo variance-reduction method. Used as standard methods, implemented from their definitions.
