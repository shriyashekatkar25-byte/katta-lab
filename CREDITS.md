## Ideas and concepts

- Ray Oldenburg, *The Great Good Place* (1989): the term "third place".
- Robert Putnam, *Bowling Alone* (2000): bonding vs. bridging social capital.
- Otis Dudley Duncan & Beverly Duncan (1955): the index of dissimilarity.
- Thomas Schelling (1971) and the agent-based modelling tradition.
- Daniel McFadden: the multinomial logit / random-utility model of choice.
- Helmut Vogel (1979): golden-angle phyllotaxis, used to seat residents around a place.
- Otto and Marie Neurath: Isotype.
